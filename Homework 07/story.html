Nana's Story

At night, Nana went where the walls could not follow.

There was always snow beneath her feet, pale blue beneath the moon.
The cold slipped between her toes and the wind touched her face so gently
she could almost believe it was real. Her hair streamed behind her,
and somewhere beyond the white hills was a place she had never seen
but somehow remembered.

She never dreamed of a door opening. She dreamed there were no doors at all.
